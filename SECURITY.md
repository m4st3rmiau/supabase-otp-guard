# Security

Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. Report it privately through
GitHub: **Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository.

Include what an attacker can do, the steps to reproduce, and the version or commit.
Bypasses of the permit, the rate limits or the destination rules are in scope, as are
ways to make the functions fail open. The limitations listed in
[docs/threat-model.md](docs/threat-model.md) are known and not vulnerabilities, but
ideas to close them are welcome as regular issues.
